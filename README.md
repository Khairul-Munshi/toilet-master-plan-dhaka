# Dhaka Toilet Master Plan — DNCC · DSCC · NCC

An interactive Leaflet.js web map documenting existing public toilets, quality
assessment, and demand-zone classification (High/Mid/Low) across Dhaka North
City Corporation, Dhaka South City Corporation, and Narayanganj City
Corporation. Built to support the RAJUK WASH Action Plan 2025.

**Live map:** _(add your GitHub Pages link here once enabled, e.g.
`https://your-username.github.io/toilet-master-plan-dhaka/`)_

## What this shows

- 610 digitized existing public toilets across all three corporations
- 48 facilities assessed as good quality
- 23 zones classified into demand tiers using a 200 m / 400 m / 600 m
  walking-distance standard (commercial, mixed-use, and residential areas
  respectively)
- 687 proposed new toilet sites and 86 proposed renovation sites
- Zoom-responsive marker clustering, corporation and phase filtering,
  fit-to-city view

## Data source & usage

Underlying survey data, zone boundaries, and demand classifications are drawn
from the **RAJUK WASH Action Plan 2025 (unpublished)**, authored by the WASH
sub-committee (WaterAid Bangladesh, Bhumijo Limited, Independent University
Bangladesh, Bangladesh Institute of Planners, UNICEF, University of Dhaka, and
representatives from DNCC, NCC, Dhaka WASA, and RAJUK). This map is shared for
portfolio and academic review purposes. Please do not redistribute or reuse
the underlying dataset without permission from the WASH sub-committee /
Bhumijo Limited.
